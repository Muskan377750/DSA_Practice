Q3:- Write a program to Transpose of a Matrix.

What is transpose?
Transpose of a matrix is the process of swapping the rows to columns. For a 2*3 matrix,

Matrix:- 
a11 a12 a13
a21 a22 a23

Transposed matrix:- 
a11 a21
a12 a22
a13 a23
